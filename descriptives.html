Descriptives

For each country and dataset: the distribution of building density and wealth, and how density persists across neighbours (space) and over time (2016 → 2023).

About these figures

  • Building density — histograms of density under two transforms: asinh keeps zero-building hexes; log drops them. (Google v3 contains only built hexes, so it has no zeros; the 2.5D grid does, so the two histograms differ there.)
  • Wealth — distribution of predicted IWI, with the poverty line (IWI 35) marked.
  • Spatial persistence — own asinh density vs the mean of its 6 H3 neighbours; a tight diagonal = strong spatial autocorrelation.
  • Temporal persistence — asinh density in 2016 vs 2023 (2.5D panel, 12 countries); the slope is an AR(1) persistence coefficient.

Each persistence view shows the raw scatter (8k-hex sample) beside a 10×10 binned count heatmap of the same relationship. Density = arcsinh(buildings / km²) at H3 resolution 7 (~5 km).
