About these figures

Pick a variable (top row); where a variable has more than one view, a second row of sub-tabs appears. Every picture is a single figure, shown for every country at once — nothing combines panels.

  • Building density — histogram of density, with two sub-tabs: asinh keeps zero-building hexes (so the empty-vs-settled split is visible); log drops them. (Google v3 contains only built hexes, so it has no zeros; the 2.5D grid does, so the two differ there.)
  • Wealth — distribution of predicted IWI, with the poverty line (IWI 35) marked.
  • Spatial persistence — own density vs the mean of its 6 H3 neighbours; sub-tabs scatter (raw 8k-hex sample) and heatmap. A tight diagonal = strong spatial autocorrelation.
  • Temporal persistence — density in 2016 vs 2023 (2.5D panel, 12 countries), as a scatter (slope = AR(1) persistence) and a heatmap.

Density = arcsinh(buildings / km²) at H3 resolution 7 (~5 km). Heatmaps use within-country decile ranks on both axes (1 = lowest 10%, 10 = highest), so each panel runs rank 1→10 regardless of the country's absolute density; cell colour is the number of hexes (darker = more), and a dark diagonal means rank tends to stay put.
