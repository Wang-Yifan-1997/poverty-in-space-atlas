Pick a dataset and a variable — see that figure for every country at once. Distributions of building density and wealth, plus how density persists across neighbours (space) and over time (2016 → 2023).

About these figures

Every tab is a single figure, shown for every country at once. Views that used to combine panels are now split into their own tabs so each picture carries one idea.

  • Density (asinh) — histogram of density under asinh, which keeps zero-building hexes (so the empty-vs-settled split is visible).
  • Density (log) — the same density under log, which drops zeros. (Google v3 contains only built hexes, so it has none; the 2.5D grid does, so the two histograms differ there.)
  • Wealth — distribution of predicted IWI, with the poverty line (IWI 35) marked.
  • Spatial · scatter / heatmap — own asinh density vs the mean of its 6 H3 neighbours, as a raw 8k-hex scatter and as a 10×10 binned count heatmap; a tight diagonal = strong spatial autocorrelation.
  • Temporal · scatter / heatmap — asinh density in 2016 vs 2023 (2.5D panel, 12 countries); the scatter's slope is an AR(1) persistence coefficient, the heatmap its binned density.

Density = arcsinh(buildings / km²) at H3 resolution 7 (~5 km).
