Methodology

Each point is a resolution-7 H3 hexagon (~5 km across). Seven Google Open Buildings footprint features per hexagon — density, built-up fraction, mean and spread of footprint size, and small/medium/large size shares — feed gradient-boosted models trained on 7,442 DHS survey clusters across 12 African countries. Everything is buildings-only (no nightlights), so any country maps from its footprint file alone. Country borders (ADM0) are drawn on every map.

Coverage toggle: “Screened” shows only hexes with ≥10 buildings (the model's training floor, where footprint-shape features are stable); “All buildings” shows every populated hex but is noisier. Colours use one fixed scale per indicator across all countries. Predictions are model estimates, not official statistics — most reliable in Sub-Saharan Africa; North Africa and small islands are extrapolations, and extreme poverty (a rare tail) is the least accurate indicator.

Temporal panel (2016 & 2023). The Period selector also offers two years from the Google Open Buildings 2.5D product for 12 countries with a building panel. That product carries only count, density, built-up fraction and building height — no footprint-size features — so its models are retrained on what exists in every year and are a little less accurate (wealth R² = 0.50 vs 0.60). Scales are fixed across countries and both years, so the 2016 and 2023 maps are directly comparable.
